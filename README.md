# Harbor Fishing

Drop a line off a Maine lobster boat and see how deep you can fish. Steer your hook around the fish on the way down, then catch as many as you can on the way back up. Sell your haul, buy a longer line and a bigger catch, and work your way from the harbor shallows down to the anglerfish and giant squid of the deep.

- One-finger fishing: steer the hook left and right
- 18 kinds of Gulf of Maine sea life, from herring down to the giant squid, plus rare finds like the Blue Lobster and the Golden Mackerel
- Upgrade your line, your catch size and your crew's idle income
- Your crew keeps fishing while you're away

`index.html` is the complete game (one file, CrazyGames HTML5 SDK v3). `covers/` and `videos/` hold the store art.
